Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aisultan Bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, and handles information when you use the Bot on Discord.

1. Information We Collect

Aisultan Bot may process the following information:

  • Discord User ID.
  • Discord username.
  • Discord channel ID.
  • Discord server ID.
  • Messages sent in the configured game channel for checking answers.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Aisultan Bot is used to:

  • Operate the Guess the Level game.
  • Check player answers.
  • Identify the player who guessed the correct level.
  • Manage active game sessions.
  • Allow users to start new games.

3. Data Storage

Aisultan Bot does not currently use a permanent database to store player information or game statistics.

Active game information is temporarily stored in the Bot's memory and is removed when a game ends or the Bot restarts.

The Bot does not currently maintain a permanent player collection.

4. Third-Party Services

Aisultan Bot operates through Discord and uses Discord's API.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy.

Aisultan Bot does not control how Discord processes your personal information.

5. Data Sharing

Aisultan Bot does not sell or intentionally share collected information with third parties.

Information may be processed by Discord as necessary for the Bot to operate.

6. Children's Privacy

Aisultan Bot does not intentionally collect additional personal information from children.

Users must follow Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time.

Continued use of Aisultan Bot after changes means that you acknowledge the updated Privacy Policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Bot owner.
